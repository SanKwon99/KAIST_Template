Card: a transparent, corner-marked line drawing for grouping a short item (kicker, title, body, meta) — never a filled or rounded block.

```jsx
<Card kicker="Getting started" title="Pick a direction" meta="Updated today">
  Three variants of the current theme, one axis at a time.
</Card>
```

- `elevation="sm|md|lg"` adds a navy-tinted shadow (use sparingly).
- `metaIcon` takes an inline Lucide SVG at 13px.
